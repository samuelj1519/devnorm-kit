# 5.1 User tasks and flows

State the situation in which the user starts, the result they want, and how they know they are done; include preparation before the start, handoffs across pages or channels, and recovery after failure. For example, a booking flow is specified from finding a suitable slot through a retrievable booking result, including self-service changes and cancellation.

Identify frequent tasks, first-time tasks, and rare high-risk tasks. Shorten repeated input, choices, and page hops on frequent paths; decisions that matter are made by the user, and reliable known information is filled in by the system.

Validate needs through interviews, observation, usage data, or prototypes where possible. When problems appear, simplify the flow, defaults, and information organization before adding help. Task cards and observation methods are in [UI acceptance](5.8-ui-acceptance.md).
