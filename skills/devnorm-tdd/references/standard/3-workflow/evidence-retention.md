# Records and evidence

Update the original authoritative task record with this increment, preserving implementer and primary-reviewer attribution: exact versions, commands, exit codes, initial failures and reruns, unverified scope, next owner, and necessary lessons. Do not repeatedly transcribe facts available from original records. Sufficient existing records do not require parallel development, acceptance, and review reports or an extra completion report. Preserve formal acceptance receipts and required signatures. Direct work needs only a brief result. An ordinary review adds only its conclusion and exact scope, confirmed findings, personally executed or cited evidence, and unverified limits, linking existing facts. Checklists guide coverage; they do not require copying each rule, normal result, or irrelevant N/A. Required unverified items, failures, and independent attribution still remain explicit.

Keep raw output separate from summaries. Prefer native runner summaries, exit codes, and failure locations. Read scoped portions of large logs, mark truncation or redaction, and link originals. Do not load every log into model context or add paid summarization by default. Summaries navigate evidence; they cannot hide required failures, replay obligations, or actual executors.

Feed rework, review findings, post-merge regressions, architecture violations, and duplicate-code growth into existing quality statistics. Keep unknowns unknown without adding a ledger for every ordinary repair. The record owner maintains the current conclusion and evidence links. Handover adds only changes, risks, and the next action; acknowledging it creates no new approval.

Before cleanup, verify exact paths, ownership, later consumers, references, retention conditions, and authorization. Evidence for active tasks, unresolved issues, critical failures, model boundaries, and final acceptance is formally retained. Referenced temporary material receives the same protection. Task closure or elapsed days does not automatically end retention. Retain material when policy is absent or continued use is uncertain. Verify archive access, index, and originals before authorized removal of rebuildable temporary copies. Keep others' resources, active resources, and unique evidence.
