# 3.3 Task brief and work allocation

Use the [short brief](../../../assets/templates/task-short.md) for ordinary tasks and the [full brief](../../../assets/templates/task.md) for high-risk tasks to fix the goal, starting version, inputs, allowed files, implementer, reviewer, interface owner, checks, and stop conditions. Check directory, branch, and existing changes. Assign one writer to shared interfaces, dependency locks, and generated output. Separate source, test data, and build artifacts.

Estimate work, task relationships, and parallel opportunities before comparing direct work with delegation. Include briefing, execution, receiving, understanding, integration, review, and rework. Label estimates and unknowns. Do not delegate work that requires extensive reinterpretation or reconstruction on return. Small tasks, tightly coupled work, and overall design usually belong to one executor. Keep required independent review.

For necessary delegation, check capabilities, tools, quota, and project limits. Agree on [return conditions](../../../assets/templates/task.md#return-conditions-for-delegated-work). Stop writing and preserve the situation after repeated failed repairs, a need to change unapproved files, or ambiguous requirements. The coordinator decides whether to revise the plan, implementation, or environment. Obtain authorization for out-of-scope actions. Repeated returns require a different split or executor. Review total cost and quality two weeks after adoption. For insufficient quota, update progress and arrange a replacement here.

| Check arrangement | Task requirements | Merge conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary standard task | Check scope, version, permissions, and affected behavior. Obtain a brief independent review. | Full checks for the exact merge candidate. |
| High-risk standard task | All applicable checks, real critical behavior, and full independent review. | Full checks and required runtime acceptance for the exact version. |
| Evidence reuse for identical inputs, with consolidated milestone checks | Confirm unchanged sources, rules, dependencies, samples, tools, and environment. Preserve original logs and explicitly label reuse. | Prepare an isolated combined version, complete consolidated full checks, then merge. |

Choose ordinary or high-risk arrangements from the change facts in section 3.1, not technology names alone. Reference agreed project waiting/rework limits; establish missing applicable limits before relying on them, without copying model-example numbers. Rework review may focus on changed and affected areas but must replay the original failure under section 3.6. A small standard-workflow change still requires independent review, unlike direct work in section 3.1. Other reductions need separate approval.
