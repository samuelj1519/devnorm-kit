# 6.7 Sensitive information and cleanup

Revoke or rotate exposed credentials first. Within authorization, address repository history, caches, branches, and other copies, coordinating with maintainers or platform support where needed. Deleting the current file, adding `.gitignore`, or force-pushing once does not prove all historical copies are removed. Do not copy secrets into reports or command records.

Ignore reproducible temporary output by default. Archive unique acceptance evidence under project policy and preserve its original contents, not just a digest. Untracking does not remove history. Before removing a branch, worktree, or directory, verify ownership, remaining changes, unpushed commits, and future users. Clean only authorized resources no longer in use; do not forcibly remove worktrees containing unknown content.
