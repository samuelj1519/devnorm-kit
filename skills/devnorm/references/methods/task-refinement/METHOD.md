# Task refinement

**Run only when the prompt explicitly requests deeper framing, a rewritten task prompt, or a research-depth check.** Do not expand an ordinary small task into a larger project.

Check whether the question uses relevant primary material, an appropriate professional method, and independently testable completion criteria. Distinguish greater depth from extra scope, data access, or paid tools. Propose the smallest useful improvement and expected benefit. A rewritten prompt must preserve the original goal and authorization and identify unresolved choices.

Complete when scope, inputs, method, and acceptance are clear. Propose out-of-scope data, tools, or costs without executing them. A suggestion is not an accepted replacement task.

Source: adapted from pinned pstack sharpen. Keeps primary material, professional methods, and independent verification without unrelated expansion.
